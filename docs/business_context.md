# Business context

The following is an explicit project assumption, not an externally verified fact.

BudgetFitzz is treated as an Instagram-first affordable men's fashion discovery and styling brand in Delhi NCR, India. The assumed primary audience is Indian men aged approximately 18-30, especially college students, early-career professionals, and price-sensitive buyers. The assumed voice is direct, practical, energetic, modern, budget-conscious, and non-pretentious. The commercial objective is qualified engagement, saves, shares, profile actions, link-request comments, affiliate-product interest, and repeat audience growth. The content objective is to become useful enough to save and share rather than publishing predominantly repetitive promotions.

## User-reported Instagram discovery observation

- **Evidence type:** user-reported qualitative observation
- **Observation:** The project owner reports finding BudgetFitzz on Instagram while actively searching and comparing multiple fashion products.
- **Interpretation:** This is a positive discovery signal: BudgetFitzz surfaced during a high-intent product-research journey, suggesting that searchable product and styling content can contribute to account discovery.
- **Limitation:** This is one self-reported journey. It does not establish Instagram search ranking, population-level discoverability, reach, or conversion performance.
- **Recommended validation:** Retain clear product-category and styling language in captions and on-screen copy, then validate the signal with Instagram discovery-source insights, profile visits, follows, product or link actions, and a short 'How did you find us?' response field.
