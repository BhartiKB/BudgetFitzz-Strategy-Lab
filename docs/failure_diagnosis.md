# Failure diagnosis

The failed baseline is best explained by content-system imbalance: 90% promotional and 91.3% static output concentrated in two topics, reinforced by repetitive or missing CTAs. The issue is not that every promotion underperformed; rather, the mix left too little room for the educational, saveable, community, and retention-led formats that can build repeat demand.

## Counterevidence

- Promotion median engagement remains close to the overall median, so promotion is not intrinsically ineffective.
- Video median engagement (6.45%) is only slightly above post median (6.30%).
- The community pillar has only two observations and is anecdotal, not proof of superiority.

## Outlier caveat

The video mean is 11.13% but falls to 6.56% after removing one extreme video. This is directional evidence for testing video, not a causal claim.
