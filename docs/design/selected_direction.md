# Selected direction: Editorial Creator Atelier

The final direction treats BudgetFitzz as a creator workspace with an editorial conscience. Media and captions are primary; analytics explain decisions without turning the product into a generic dashboard.

Core choices:

- Nine focused routes instead of one long anchor page.
- Observed, target, and hypothesis states use distinct labels and treatments.
- Every visible metric comes from the pipeline-generated platform manifest.
- A weekly plan is the operating center, with exactly five posts and two videos.
- Studio previews keep media, caption, CTA, KPI, and agent rationale together.
- Desktop uses a compact side rail; mobile uses bottom navigation and sheets.
- Warm paper surfaces, fine lines, restrained shadow, Newsreader/Inter hierarchy.
