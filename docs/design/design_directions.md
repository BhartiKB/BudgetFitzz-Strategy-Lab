# Stitch direction review

| Direction | Strength | Risk | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial Social Studio | Strongest brand voice, warm palette, clear evidence hierarchy | Too much unused desktop space; weak planning density | Retain visual language |
| Instagram Creator Workspace | Best planning flow, previews, filters, captioning and agent feedback | Rose/lavender treatment is less distinctive and can lose contrast | Retain interaction model |
| Fashion Analytics Magazine | Excellent long-form analytical storytelling | Feels like a report rather than an everyday creator tool; very long page | Use only selective editorial discipline |

The selected system is a deliberate hybrid: Direction 2's operating model, Direction 1's editorial voice, and Direction 3's strict separation of evidence from interpretation.
