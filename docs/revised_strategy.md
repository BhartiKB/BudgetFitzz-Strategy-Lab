# Revised strategy

## Objective

Increase qualified saves, shares, profile actions, link-request comments, and repeat audience growth by making BudgetFitzz immediately useful.

## Evidence-linked changes

| Evidence | Finding | Change | Success metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | Promotion occupies 90.0% of the baseline. | Cap promotional/value-first commercial items at 2 of 7; allocate 3 education and 2 community items. | Pillar mix compliance; education median save rate; qualitative comment coding. |
| E03 | Education median save rate is 0.522% vs promotion 0.232%. | Make the takeaway visible on-frame: formulas, checklists, comparisons, and avoidable mistakes. | Save rate target 0.45-0.70% on education posts. |
| E02,E04,E05 | Posts are 91.3% of the baseline; video has n=13 and one extreme outlier. | Use exactly two short videos with first-second hooks, 3-scene pacing, captions, and explicit end cards. | Retention proxy target 60-70% vs observed median 55.2%. |
| E06 | Outfit Ideas and Budget Finds account for 66.7% of all records. | Add wardrobe systems, fit diagnosis, colour voting, and wardrobe-audit topics while retaining familiar commercial anchors. | No topic repeated more than twice in the seven-day cycle. |
| E07 | Fourteen CTAs are missing and two rows use @aristostyling. | Use one primary CTA per asset, rotate save/share/vote/link-request intents, and standardize @budgetfitzz. | 100% CTA coverage; 0 wrong-handle occurrences; at least 4 CTA styles across 7 items. |

## Measurement

- Engagement rate by reach
- Save rate
- Share rate
- Comment quality
- Average watch time
- Retention proxy
- Profile actions
- Link-request comments
- Pillar balance
- Publishing consistency
